2b. SOS call
The SOS ally is picked from its own RNG (seed and frame from 3DS RNG Tool’s tools), while EC, PID, IVs, nature and gender come from the main RNG. The ally slots above follow 3DS RNG Tool’s rules for the Pokémon you pick. Correction isn’t used for SOS calls.
2b. Fishing
Pick Suction Cups as lead to always get a bite. Frames where you hook an item instead of a Pokémon are skipped. Delay can be negative here (Lapras spots start at 0, others at -2).
2b. Special encounter
2. DexNav
This is the main-RNG part of a DexNav encounter, as 3DS RNG Tool computes it without a timeline: EC, PID, IVs, ability, nature and gender. The Pokémon’s slot, the shaking spot and the TinyMT side need the tool’s timeline and aren’t included. Match Potential to the stars shown on the DexNav.
2. Horde
This is the main-RNG part of a horde, as 3DS RNG Tool computes it without a timeline: each frame gives all 5 Pokémon (EC, PID, IVs, ability, nature, gender). The hidden-ability position and whether Synchronize triggers come from the TinyMT side, so enter what the game shows. Pick your game and route to see its three hordes; tapping one sets the species and gender ratio of each position. Without a route, one gender ratio is used for all 5.

Gen 7 seeds use the same format as 3DS RNG Tool: “Seed here” is the egg seed at that frame, “Seed after” is the seed once you have accepted that egg. Gen 6 uses your 32-bit initial seed and a frame range; the delay field mirrors the tool (16 is its default when accepting without Shiny Charm or Masuda Method).
